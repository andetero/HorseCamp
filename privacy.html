Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026 · Effective: August 13, 2026

Plain English summary: HorseCamp does not require an account and does not run a user database for your horse records. Rig, horse, document, and trip information is stored locally on your iPhone or Android device. The app connects to outside services for campground data, weather, wildfire context, maps, geocoding, and directions. Camp submissions and issue reports are sent only when you choose to submit them. Shared trip links can be viewed by anyone who receives the link.

Who operates HorseCamp

HorseCamp is developed and operated by Pyoba LLC, a Colorado limited liability company. HorseCampFinder.com is the public website and data endpoint used by the HorseCamp mobile apps.

What HorseCamp collects

HorseCamp does not require an account. Standard app use does not ask for a username, password, personal email address, or payment information. HorseCamp does not operate a cloud account database for your horse records or trip plans.

Data typeHow it is handled
LocationUsed when permitted for nearby results, maps, weather, wildfire context, directions, and nearby equine services. HorseCamp does not maintain a location-history database.
Horse records and documentsStored locally in app-specific device storage, subject to your device backup settings. Original document files are not uploaded to a HorseCamp document server.
Trip plansStored locally unless you choose to share a trip link.
Camp submissions and issue reportsSent to HorseCamp's review service only when you submit them.
AnalyticsHorseCamp does not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.
Crash diagnosticsApple or Google platform diagnostic services may process crash information depending on your device settings.

What the app connects to

HorseCamp uses network services to provide current travel information. Depending on the feature, the app may connect to HorseCampFinder.com, HorseCamp's review service, and public weather, wildfire, map, geocoding, and directions services. External websites or apps may also open when you choose to view campground websites, directions, phone numbers, email addresses, or shared trip links.

These services may receive ordinary technical information needed to fulfill a request, such as your IP address, requested location or coordinates, map search text, or the URL you open. HorseCamp does not sell personal data or use advertising networks.

Location

If you grant location permission, HorseCamp can use your location for nearby campground results, map context, weather and wildfire context, directions-related features, and nearby equine services. You can deny or revoke location permission in your device settings.

Your horse documents

Coggins certificates, health certificates, brand inspection records, vaccination records, and other files you add are stored locally in app-specific storage. If you enable iCloud Backup, Android/Google device backup, or another device backup service, eligible app data may be backed up according to that service's settings.

Trip sharing

If you choose to share a trip, the shared link can include the trip name, origin, destination, departure date, planned stops, overnight camp stops, and route advisories included by the app. Anyone with the link can view the itinerary. HorseCamp does not create a user account when you share a trip.

No accounts, no sign-in

HorseCamp has no account system on iPhone or Android. If accounts, subscriptions, analytics, reviews, or cloud sync are added in the future, this policy will be updated before those features are shipped.

Children's privacy

HorseCamp is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party services

Apple, Google, device map services, public weather/wildfire/geographic services, and websites you choose to open are governed by their own privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

If what HorseCamp collects or how app data is stored or shared changes, this policy and its effective date will be updated.

Email support@horsecampfinder.com with privacy questions.
