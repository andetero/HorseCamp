Common questions

Frequently asked.

How do I add a missing campground?⌄
Use Submit a Camp in HorseCamp and provide the campground information you know. Submissions are reviewed before they are added to the public campground feed. If you cannot use the in-app form, email support@horsecampfinder.com.
How do I report incorrect campground information?⌄
Open the campground in HorseCamp and choose Report an Issue. Tell us what needs to be corrected, such as hookups, horse facilities, seasonal information, location, website, or a campground that no longer allows horses.
How do I add my horse's Coggins or other documents?⌄
Go to Profile, select your horse, then add the appropriate document type. HorseCamp supports horse records such as Coggins certificates, health certificates, brand inspection records, and vaccination documents. Expiration reminders are available when notifications are enabled.
Can I share horse documents from HorseCamp?⌄
Original horse document files remain in app-specific storage on your device and are not uploaded to a HorseCamp document server. Use your device's normal sharing controls when you choose to export or send a file.
What states require a brand inspection or health certificate?⌄
Requirements vary by state, destination, and type of movement and can change. HorseCamp helps you organize records but does not determine legal entry requirements. Confirm current requirements with the applicable state livestock, agriculture, or animal-health authority before hauling.
How do campground filters work?⌄
Set your rig information in HorseCamp and use available filters for details such as rig length, electrical hookups, stalls, paddocks, and other campground features. Source data varies, so always verify final fit, availability, and horse rules with the campground.
How do I find vets or farriers?⌄
Use HorseCamp's nearby equine-services feature to find vets and farriers and open available map or directions results. Availability and business details come from external map/search services and should be confirmed directly.
Do I need an account?⌄
No. HorseCamp does not require a user account on iPhone or Android. Rig, horse, document, and trip information is stored locally on your device unless you choose to share it or your device backup settings copy eligible app data.
Is HorseCamp free?⌄
Yes. HorseCamp is free on iPhone and Android and does not require an in-app subscription.
