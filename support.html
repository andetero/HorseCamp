Common questions

Frequently asked.

How do I upload my horse's coggins certificate?
Go to Profile, select your horse, then add a Coggins Certificate or another document type. You can attach a photo or PDF from your phone, or use the camera when available. HorseCamp can remind you before documents expire when you allow notifications.
What states require a brand inspection certificate?
Brand inspection, health certificate, ownership, and entry requirements vary by state and can change. HorseCamp is a planning tool and cannot determine whether a particular certificate is required for your trip. Confirm current requirements directly with the applicable state livestock, agriculture, or animal-health authority before hauling.
Can I share horse documents from HorseCamp?
HorseCamp keeps original horse document files local to your device and does not upload them to a HorseCamp document server. Use your device's normal sharing controls when you choose to export or send a file. Device backup settings may also apply to eligible app data.
Is HorseCamp free to use?
Yes. HorseCamp is free on iPhone and Android and does not require a HorseCamp account or in-app subscription. You can browse camps, save horse information on your device, and plan trips.
How do I filter camps by my rig length?
Set up your rig once during onboarding or in Profile. Enter your trailer type, total rig length including truck, and preferred electrical hookup. Camp listings may include maximum rig length and hookup notes when the source provides them. Always verify final fit and availability directly with the campground before hauling.
How do I list my camp, vet practice, or farrier business?
Email support@horsecampfinder.com with your business name, location, website, phone number, and the horse-specific services you offer. We review submitted corrections and listing requests manually so riders see accurate information.
How do I report incorrect camp information?
Email support@horsecampfinder.com with the camp name, state, and the correction you want us to review. Useful details include hookup changes, closed stalls, wrong seasonal dates, bad links, or places that no longer allow horses.
Do I need to create an account?
No. HorseCamp does not have user accounts on iPhone or Android. Your rig, horse, document, and trip information is stored locally on your device unless you choose to share it or your device backup settings copy eligible data.
Is my horse's health data secure?
HorseCamp stores original horse document files locally in app-specific device storage and does not run a HorseCamp document server or account database for them. Protect your phone with a device passcode or biometric lock, and review your iCloud, Google, or other device-backup settings for how eligible app data is backed up.
