Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026  ·  Effective: July 6, 2026

🔒

Plain English summary: HorseCamp does not require an account and does not run a user database for your horse records. Your rig, horse, document, and trip information is stored locally on your iPhone or Android device. The app connects to public services for campground data, weather, wildfire context, maps, and directions. When you choose to submit a camp or report a listing issue, the information you enter is sent to HorseCamp's review service. Shared trip links can be viewed by anyone who receives the link.

What HorseCamp collects

HorseCamp does not require an account. Standard app use does not ask for a username, password, personal email address, or payment information. We do not operate a HorseCamp account database for your horse records or trip plans. A camp listing submission can include business contact information, listing details, and optional photo URLs when you choose to provide them.

What the app connects to

HorseCamp uses network services so the app can show current and useful travel information. Depending on what you tap and what permissions you grant, the app may connect to:

  • HorseCampFinder.com to download public campground data and display shared trip pages.
  • HorseCamp's review service when you submit a missing camp or report a listing issue.
  • Public weather, wildfire, map, geocoding, and directions services to show requested travel information and map context.
  • External websites and apps when you choose to open campground websites, trail links, directions, phone calls, emails, or shared trip links.

These services may receive ordinary technical information needed to fulfill the request, such as your IP address, approximate/requested location, map search text, coordinates, or the URL you open. HorseCamp does not use advertising networks or sell personal data.

Location

If you grant location permission, HorseCamp uses your location to show nearby camps, nearby equine services, map context, weather context, and directions-related features. You can deny or revoke location permission in your iPhone or Android device settings. Some features may be less useful without location access.

Your horse health documents

Coggins certificates, health certificates, brand inspection records, vaccination records, and other files you add are stored locally in app-specific storage on your device. HorseCamp does not upload original document files to a HorseCamp account or document server.

  • Original document files remain local unless you separately export or share them through your device.
  • Deleting a document in the app removes the app's local copy. A separate device backup may retain a backup copy according to the backup provider's settings and retention period.
  • If you enable iCloud Backup, Android/Google device backup, or another device backup service, eligible app data may be backed up to the account or service you choose.

Trip sharing

If you choose to share a trip, the shared link can include the trip name, origin, destination, departure date, and planned overnight stops. Anyone with the link can view the itinerary. Share links are intended only for people you choose to send them to.

HorseCamp does not create a user account or a HorseCamp cloud copy of your trip when you use a shared link.

No accounts, no sign-in

HorseCamp has no account system on iPhone or Android. You do not create a username or password. If we add accounts, subscriptions, reviews, analytics, or cloud sync in a future version, we will update this policy before shipping that version.

Children's privacy

HorseCamp is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party services

HorseCamp does not use advertising SDKs, social login, or third-party analytics SDKs. Apple, Google, device map services, public weather/wildfire/geographic services, and websites you choose to open are governed by their own privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

If we change what data we collect or how app data is stored or shared, we will update this policy and revise the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@horsecampfinder.com. We'll respond within a few days.
