Everything you need on the road

Built for haulers, not just campers.

🚛

Find camps that fit your rig

Filter by rig length, 30A or 50A hookups, stalls, paddocks, sewer, and other horse-first campground details when the source provides them.

🗺️

Plan multi-stop trips

Build trips across all 50 states, add planned stops, compare campground options, and save your itinerary for the road.

📄

Keep horse documents handy

Store Coggins certificates, health certificates, brand inspection records, and vaccine documents locally on your device, with expiration reminders when notifications are enabled.

🐴

Camp notes for haulers

Review available information about stalls, corrals, paddocks, hookups, rig length, bathhouses, dump stations, seasonal notes, and source links before arrival.

🩺

Find vets & farriers

Find nearby equine vets and farriers when you need help on the road, with quick access to maps and directions.

🔥

Wildfire & trip weather

Check active wildfires near your trip and review weather along the way before you haul. Always verify current road and emergency conditions with official local sources.

Getting started

On the trail in minutes.

01 · Set up your rig

Save trailer type, total rig length, and hookup preferences so campground information is easier to compare.

02 · Add your horses

Keep each horse's documents and expiration dates organized in one place on your device.

03 · Search & plan

Find camps nearby or along a trip, add planned stops, and review weather and wildfire context.

04 · Haul prepared

Keep your itinerary and horse records handy and use the campground's source information to verify final conditions.
